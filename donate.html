Item donations

What we accept

We accept a wide range of good-condition household items. Everything must be clean, in working order, and ready to use.

Real items donated to SAOK and redistributed to families across Wolverhampton.

✓ We do accept

  • Beds, mattresses, and bed frames
  • Sofas, armchairs, and dining sets
  • Wardrobes, chests of drawers, shelving
  • Duvets, pillows, and bedding
  • Towels and bathroom essentials
  • Pots, pans, plates, cutlery, and kitchen goods
  • Clothing for adults and children (all sizes)
  • Toiletries and personal care items
  • Children's toys, books, and games
  • Cleaning products and household supplies
  • Small kitchen appliances in working order

✗ We cannot accept

  • Items that are damaged, broken, or heavily worn
  • Items with stains, tears, or strong odours
  • Mattresses without a fire safety label
  • Electrical items that are not in working order
  • Gas appliances of any kind
  • Items that have not been cleaned
  • Baby car seats (for safety reasons)
  • Medication or medical equipment

A simple rule of thumb

If you would be happy giving the item to a friend or family member, we would be happy to receive it. If you would not, please consider disposing of it responsibly rather than donating it.

Collection & drop-off

How to get items to us

1

Get in touch

Contact us via our contact page and let us know what items you have. Include a brief description and, if possible, a photo so we can confirm we can take them.

2

We arrange collection or drop-off

We will arrange a convenient time to collect from your home, workplace, or business. Alternatively, you are welcome to drop items directly to one of our storage units - we will confirm the address when you get in touch.

3

We check and store your items

Every donated item is checked to make sure it is clean and in good condition before being stored and prepared for delivery to families.

4

Your items reach a family

When a referral comes in, your donated items are matched to a family's needs and delivered to their new home - sometimes within hours. Your generosity makes a direct, immediate difference.

For businesses

Partner with us

We welcome support from businesses of all sizes. Whether you have surplus stock to clear, want to engage your team in a volunteering day, or are looking for a local charity to support as part of your CSR programme - we would love to hear from you.

Surplus stock clearance

Hotels, retailers, and manufacturers regularly refurbish or clear stock. We can collect good-condition items quickly and put them straight to use.

Staff fundraising

Set up a workplace giving scheme, a dress-down day, or a team fundraising challenge. We will provide everything you need to make it a success.

CSR & reporting

We provide a letter of thanks, a verified certificate, and impact reporting so you can include your support in your CSR and sustainability reports.
