Christmas campaign

Give a Gift & Make a Smile This Christmas

We are partnering with the RAFA Association to collect Christmas gifts for children who might not otherwise receive one this year. Wrapped or unwrapped gifts are welcome - if wrapped, please label with the child's gender and age.

All gifts collected are passed to Simple Acts of Kindness for distribution to families in need across Wolverhampton.

Drop-off location: RAFA Association, 26 Goldthorn Road, Wolverhampton WV2 4PN
Autumn Fair: Saturday 17th October, 12 - 4pm
Christmas Fair: Saturday 14th November, 12 - 4pm
Regular drop-off: Mondays 7.30 - 10pm & Wednesdays 9.30 - 11am

🎁 Donation cut-off: Monday 30th November (due to distribution). If you know of a child who would benefit from receiving a gift, please contact us in the strictest confidence.
