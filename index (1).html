What we do

The simple things that make a home

When a family escapes domestic abuse, they often leave with nothing. No cutlery. No bedding. No way to cook a meal. We hold donated items across two storage units and can deliver a full household starter kit within 24 hours of a referral.

Beds & bedding

Mattresses, frames, duvets and pillows so a family can sleep safely on night one.

Kitchen essentials

Pots, pans, cutlery, plates — everything needed to cook a first meal at home.

Clothing & toiletries

Clean clothes and personal care items, sorted by size for adults and children.

Furniture

Sofas, tables, chairs — so families have somewhere to sit together.
